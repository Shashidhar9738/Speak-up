What each role can see
Owner (CXO)
Every report including harassment and security. Manages accounts. Exports.
Reviewer (HR / Compliance)
Every report including sensitive ones. Can reply and export. Cannot manage accounts.
Department lead
Only their own departments. Harassment and security reports are hidden — those often name the lead.
Analyst
Counts, categories and trends only. Never sees complaint text.
